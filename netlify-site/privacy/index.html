Privacy notice

Set Up & Seen respects your privacy. This notice explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address, telephone number, chosen service and the information included in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, prepare a proposal, provide requested services and keep appropriate business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Website forms and service providers

Enquiries submitted through this website are processed by the form-delivery service used by the site and sent to info@setupandseen.co.uk. Essential website hosting providers may also process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the website.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose collected and to meet legal, tax or business-record obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@setupandseen.co.uk or call 07999 071045.

Cookies

This website uses essential technology for security and reliable delivery. With your permission, we also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may set _ga and related cookies, usually for up to two years. We do not use advertising cookies, and analytics storage remains switched off unless you select “Accept analytics”. Your choice is saved on your device and can be changed at any time using the Cookie settings button.

Lawful bases

We process enquiries because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, and for our legitimate interests in responding to prospective clients and operating the business. Client and financial records may also be kept to meet legal obligations.
