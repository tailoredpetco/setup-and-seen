Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you enquire.

Do I need to supply photographs and videos?+

Yes, for the core package you provide authentic photographs, clips and updates. We turn them into eight planned content pieces, written, designed and adapted for Facebook and Instagram. Photography, filming and advanced video editing can be quoted separately.

Is paid advertising included?+

No. Organic content support and paid campaigns are separate, and any advertising budget is paid directly to the platform.

Will I approve posts first?+

Yes. You receive the content plan for approval before posts are scheduled.
