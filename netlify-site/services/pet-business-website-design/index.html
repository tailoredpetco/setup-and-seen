Answer the owner’s questions

Make your pet-care service easy to understand

Someone looking for help with their pet needs to know who you work with, where you operate and how to enquire. A useful website can explain these details before a first conversation, while showing the people and real animals behind your business.

The right service details

Explain the animals you care for, service types, coverage and any practical requirements. For grooming, make it clear when a final price depends on coat, size or condition.

Real photographs and identity

Use your own photographs and retain your existing logo when that is your preference. Agree consent for client photographs before using them.

Booking that fits your business

Offer a clear enquiry route or link to your existing booking service. Bespoke booking systems, subscriptions and integrations need a separate scope.

A useful page structure

Plan around how your customers choose care

For a small grooming business, a starting structure could be Home, Services and Prices, About, Gallery and Contact. A walking or pet-care business may need more detail about service areas, visits and how a first meeting works. The final structure depends on your services and available content.

Use accurate information about qualifications, insurance and any applicable licences. I will work with the details you supply and confirm, and clearly distinguish customer reviews from your own service descriptions.
