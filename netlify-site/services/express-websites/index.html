Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you enquire.

Can my website really be ready in less than a week?+

The build is scheduled for five working days from your confirmed slot once the agreed payment, completed questionnaire, final content, images and access are ready. You need to provide prompt feedback and approvals. Missing information or delayed feedback pauses the timetable; domain and platform processing can affect the public launch.

What if I need more than five pages?+

We can recommend a larger website package and give you a clear quote based on the additional pages and features.

Is hosting included?+

No. Domain and hosting costs are separate from the build. Optional Website Care starts from £49 a month and includes hosting, SSL, backups, checks and a small monthly content update. Your written quote confirms the full cost.
