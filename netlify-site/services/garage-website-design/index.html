Built around your services

Show customers what they can bring to you

A garage website needs to explain your actual services, the vehicles you work on and how a customer can request help. A repair enquiry, a camper enquiry and a vehicle-stock enquiry need different information. We plan the page structure around the work you want to attract.

Clear service information

Separate your main services so a driver can find the right information quickly. Include only the repairs, facilities and qualifications your business can substantiate.

Useful project photographs

Use your own workshop, vehicle and completed-work photographs where permission allows. Add captions that explain what the customer is seeing.

A practical contact route

Make your telephone number, opening hours and enquiry options easy to find on a phone. Agree any booking or vehicle-information requirements before the build.

Plan before building

A starting structure for a garage website

A five-page starting point might include Home, Services, About, Work or Gallery, and Contact. A garage with several distinct repair services may benefit from individual service pages instead. We agree the structure with you rather than promising every feature within a fixed page count.

If you need stock listings, frequent vehicle updates or a booking integration, these should be scoped separately. Explore vehicle advert management if your existing sales website already needs regular listing support.

The Managed Website Starter is £149 a month for 12 months, a total of £1,788. It includes a five-page website, one standard .co.uk domain for the first year, hosting and care during the agreement, and one small content update of up to 30 minutes each month.
