Frequently asked questions

Small business website design FAQs

How much does a small business website cost?+

Website Starter begins at £495 for up to five pages, normally completed in 2–4 weeks. The One-Day Website is £495 for one page within a booked working day once everything is ready. Express Website Set Up is £999 for a priority build of up to five pages. Managed Website Starter is £149 a month for 12 months (£1,788 total), including hosting and care during the plan. Domain and hosting costs are separate for the one-off packages. Your written proposal confirms the scope and total cost.

What platform will my website use, and will I own it?+

We choose the platform around your requirements and name it in your written proposal before you commit. The proposal explains what you can edit, any ongoing platform costs, ownership of the finished custom work and the handover or transfer arrangements. Your domain remains yours.

Will my website be set up for SEO?+

Yes. Every build includes search-friendly page structure, titles, descriptions, headings, mobile optimisation and technical foundations. Ongoing SEO can be added where needed.

Do you work outside Worcestershire?+

Yes. We work with small businesses throughout the UK by phone, video call and online. If you prefer to meet in person, we can visit your business within 50 miles of our Worcestershire base by arrangement. Maria is your direct point of contact from the first conversation through to launch.
