Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you enquire.

Does my domain still belong to me?+

Yes. Your domain remains yours. We help connect it to the website and never treat it as our property.

What counts as a small content update?+

Examples include changing text, replacing an image, updating a price or adding a short notice. Larger design or feature changes are quoted first.

Can you care for a website you did not build?+

Often, yes. We first review the platform, condition and access requirements, then confirm what we can safely support.
