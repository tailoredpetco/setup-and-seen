Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you enquire.

What is the difference between the free health check and the paid audit?+

The free homepage health check gives three brief observations on one page. The £195 Website Clarity Audit reviews up to ten key pages across desktop and mobile and includes a structured, prioritised written action plan.

Will you make the recommended changes for me?+

The audit price covers the review and action plan. If you would like us to complete any improvements, we will provide a separate clear quote before work begins.

Is this a full technical SEO audit?+

No. It includes sensible basic checks of search visibility, structure and performance, but it is designed as a broader small-business website and conversion review. A large or technically complex website may need a separate specialist SEO audit.
