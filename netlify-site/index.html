Choosing Set Up & Seen

Why not just build the website myself?+

You can, but a good business website needs more than attractive pages. We plan the structure, words, customer journey, mobile experience and enquiry points so the finished site looks credible and helps people take action.

Do you use the same template for every business?+

No. We may use trusted systems to make a site reliable and easy to manage, but the layout, styling and content direction are shaped around your business and your customers.

Do you only work with new businesses?+

Not at all. We help new businesses launch properly and established businesses improve websites, branding or social profiles that no longer reflect the quality of their work.

Do you work outside Worcestershire?+

Yes. We are based in Worcestershire and work remotely with small businesses throughout the UK.

Pricing & packages

How much does a small-business website cost?+

Our Website Starter begins at £495 and is normally completed within 2–4 weeks. Our priority Express Website Set Up is £999 and is completed within a maximum of five working days once everything required is ready. Larger websites and combined launch packages start from the prices shown above.

Are there any hidden fees?+

No. Your proposal explains what is included, any optional extras and the ongoing cost if you choose managed hosting or support.

Can I pay in instalments?+

Yes. Payment plans are available for larger projects. The timing and amounts will be agreed clearly before we begin.

Can I combine website, branding and social media?+

Yes. That is often the best way to create a consistent launch. Our Business Launch and Set Up & Seen packages bring the key pieces together for less than buying each separately.

What is included in the £195 website audit?+

The Website Clarity Audit reviews up to ten key pages on desktop and mobile. It covers first impression, messaging, trust, customer journey, calls to action and basic search visibility, followed by a prioritised written action plan.

Process & timings

Can my website really be live in less than a week?+

Yes. The Express Website Set Up is completed within a maximum of five working days once your booking payment, completed questionnaire, content, images and required access have been received. Prompt feedback is essential to keep the express timescale.

What happens after I enquire?+

We arrange a friendly, no-pressure conversation, learn what you need and send a clear proposal covering the work, timescale and price.

What do you need from me?+

We will guide you through a short questionnaire and ask for your business details, services, preferred images and any existing branding. If you need help with the words, we can refine or write them for you.

Will I see the website before it goes live?+

Yes. You will review the site and give feedback before launch. The number of revision rounds included will be stated in your proposal.

Websites, SEO & mobile

Will my website work properly on mobile phones?+

Yes. Every website is designed and checked for desktop, tablet and mobile, with readable text, simple navigation and clear enquiry buttons.

Is SEO included?+

Every build includes sensible page titles, descriptions, headings and technical foundations. Ongoing SEO, location pages and content can be quoted separately when your goals require more.

Can you guarantee that I will rank first on Google?+

No reputable provider can guarantee a first-place ranking. We build strong foundations and can recommend practical work to improve visibility over time.

Can you add online booking, payments or a shop?+

Yes. Booking systems, payment links and e-commerce can be included where suitable. We will confirm any third-party fees before you commit.

What platform will my website use, and will I own it?+

We choose the platform around the project rather than forcing every business onto the same system. Your written proposal names the platform, explains what you can edit, identifies any ongoing costs and confirms the handover or transfer arrangements. Your domain remains yours, and final approved custom work is released for your business use after full payment.

Hosting & aftercare

Will you host my website for me?+

Yes. Our Website Care plan starts from £49 a month and includes managed hosting, SSL security, backups, monitoring, checks and a small monthly content update.

Can I use a domain I already own?+

Yes. Your domain remains yours. We can connect it to the new website and help with the necessary settings.

What happens after the website launches?+

You can take over the day-to-day management or choose Website Care so we continue hosting, checking and updating it for you.

What if something stops working?+

Website Care clients can contact us directly. We will investigate issues covered by the plan and explain clearly if a third-party service or larger change is involved.

Social media support

Which social media platforms do you support?+

Our core packages focus on Facebook and Instagram, with Google Business Profile support during set-up. LinkedIn, TikTok and other platforms can be added when they suit your audience.

Do I need to supply photographs and videos?+

For the £325 monthly package, you supply authentic photographs, clips and business updates. We turn them into eight planned content pieces each month, written, designed and adapted for Facebook and Instagram. Photography, filming and advanced video editing can be quoted separately.

Do I approve posts before they are published?+

Yes. You receive the content plan for approval before posts are scheduled, so you remain comfortable with what is being said on your behalf.

Does the monthly fee include paid advertising?+

No. Organic social media support and paid advertising are separate. Any advertising budget is paid to the platform, and campaign management is quoted clearly before it begins.
