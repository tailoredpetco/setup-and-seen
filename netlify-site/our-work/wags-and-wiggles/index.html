Wags & Wiggles

A warm welcome, before the first groom. A website that introduces Paige’s one-to-one care and makes the practical details easy to find.

Visit the live websiteRead the client’s review

The business

Care with a personal touch.

Wags & Wiggles offers one-to-one dog grooming with Paige at Stonham Barns Park in Stonham Aspal, Suffolk. The website introduces that gentle approach, with photographs from the salon and space to explain how appointments work.

What I created

Designed around the business.

I designed and launched the website, bringing together the grooming packages, salon gallery, visit information, FAQs and contact options. The finished design carries the business’s cream and teal styling across the pages.

The details that matter

Helping owners take the next step.

See the care.

Real photographs from the salon and an introduction to Paige give visitors a feel for the person and place behind the service.

Find the right groom.

Clear grooming packages, visit information and FAQs help owners explore the options and prepare for an appointment.

Start a conversation.

Visible WhatsApp and telephone links make it easy to contact Paige, with a link to the Tuft app for customers who prefer it.
