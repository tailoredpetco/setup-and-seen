Campers and commercial vehicles • Project case study

Camper and van website design for Clent Hills

A separate identity and website for Clent Hills Campers & Vans, bringing vehicle sales, conversions and workshop services together without losing the connection to its sister automotive brand.

Discuss your website ↗

The brief

What the business needed

Create a sister brand that felt connected to Clent Auto Repairs without repeating the same business identity. The website needed its own service messaging and a straightforward enquiry route.

The delivered scope

What Set Up & Seen created

  • Brand identity
  • Responsive website design
  • Service messaging and enquiry journey
  • Google Business Profile and Facebook set-up
  • Launch support

This project combined several services. It is not a statement that all of these deliverables are included in the Website Starter price.

The approach

Different services, with a clear route to each

The mountain-line identity connects the business visually with Clent Auto Repairs, while the name, vehicle photography and opening message give it a separate focus. The navigation distinguishes vehicles, conversions, repairs, resprays and Raptor finishes. Visitors can start an enquiry immediately or explore the services first.

The work shown here is the published project. The live website may evolve as the business updates its services and content.

The practical decisions

A separate purpose for the sister brand

Recognisable family identity
The mountain-line logo and dark blue treatment retain a visual connection to the repair business, with a distinct name and camper imagery.
Separate buying from workshop enquiries
Vehicles, conversions, repairs and finishes have individual navigation links, helping visitors choose the relevant route.
Explain how to get in touch
The homepage offers a start-an-enquiry button, a visible telephone number and a note that viewings are by appointment.

The finished work

Explore the published website

The delivered project provided a separate branded website and supporting online profiles. It demonstrates how a related business can have its own presence while remaining part of a broader business story.
