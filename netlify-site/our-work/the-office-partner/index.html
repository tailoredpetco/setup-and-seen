Meet The Office Partner

The support behind a busy business

Led by Sarah, The Office Partner provides office support for UK sole traders and small businesses. Its services include invoicing, payment follow-ups, inbox management, diaries and general administration, with call answering by arrangement. Our work gave those services a recognisable identity and a clear introduction online.

One launch, brought together

Bringing the launch together

Sarah wanted The Office Partner to have a professional introduction, with clear branding, a website that explained its support and the essentials in place for customers to make contact. Having the work handled together gave her one point of contact for the launch. We developed the brand identity, website, social media presence and branded email as one project, so each introduction would feel like the same business.

The identity

A recognisable identity for The Office Partner

We designed the logo in deep navy and teal, with clear lettering and a distinctive symbol. We then carried the identity into the website and social materials. Repeating those elements gives prospective customers a way to recognise The Office Partner as they move between its pages, posts and conversations.

The website

Helping visitors decide what to do next

A visitor needs to understand whether a service is relevant before making an enquiry. We organised the website around practical tasks, with service descriptions, support options and pricing easy to explore. The contact page explains what to include and what happens next, with a direct email option too. Each decision supports the same purpose: helping someone understand the offer and take the next step.

Social media and email

A familiar name in every conversation

We created the social media presence and set up branded email accounts using The Office Partner’s domain. The public Facebook page introduces the support available and links to the website. The matching identity and business email address help people connect a social introduction with the business they later contact.

The finished launch

What the project delivered

Launched in September 2026, The Office Partner now has a brand identity, a live website, a social media presence and branded email accounts. Sarah has the launch materials in place to introduce the business, while prospective customers can explore the services and see how to enquire. We’re pleased to welcome The Office Partner to our portfolio and share the work behind its launch.
