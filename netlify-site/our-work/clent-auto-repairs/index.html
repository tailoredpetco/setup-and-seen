Automotive repair • Project case study

Garage website design for Clent Auto Repairs

A brand and website for an automotive repair business in Halesowen, with repair information, local context and a clear choice between calling the workshop and requesting a quote.

Discuss your website ↗

The brief

What the business needed

The brief was to launch a new automotive repair business with a professional identity and a website ready to introduce it to the local market.

The delivered scope

What Set Up & Seen created

  • Brand strategy and logo design
  • Website planning and copy
  • Responsive website design
  • Google Business Profile and Facebook set-up
  • Launch support

This project combined several services. It is not a statement that all of these deliverables are included in the Website Starter price.

The approach

Repair information and workshop contact, easy to find

The homepage introduces accident damage, bodywork, diagnostics and ADAS calibration in its opening copy. Services, accident repair, standards and FAQs have their own navigation links, so drivers can explore the information relevant to their vehicle. The workshop telephone number appears in the header, with a call option beside the quote button.

The work shown here is the published project. The live website may evolve as the business updates its services and content.

The practical decisions

Helping a driver take the next step

Explain the repair work
The opening text names the main areas of work. Separate navigation links let a driver explore services and accident repair in more detail.
Establish the local connection
Halesowen and the West Midlands appear above the headline, placing the business for someone looking for a nearby workshop.
Offer a choice of contact
Request a quote and call buttons sit together below the introduction, with the workshop number repeated in the header.

The finished work

Explore the published website

The delivered work established a website, brand identity and supporting profiles. The published portfolio also records ongoing weekly website, Google and Facebook management.
