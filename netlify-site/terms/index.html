Your proposal will describe the deliverables, total price, whether VAT applies, payment schedule, timescale, included revisions and any third-party costs. Timings and the express service

The Website Starter is normally completed within 2–4 weeks after the requirements above are complete. The Express Website Set Up is completed within a maximum of five working days once those same requirements are complete and the agreed express slot begins.
