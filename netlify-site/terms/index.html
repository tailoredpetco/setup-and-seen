Website and service terms · last updated 1 August 2026

Terms

These terms explain how this website may be used and the basis on which Set Up & Seen supplies creative and digital services. A written proposal will confirm the scope, price and timescale for each project.

Quotes and payments

Prices shown on this website are starting prices. Your written proposal will state the final price, payment stages and any third-party costs. Work begins after the agreed booking payment and required information have been received.

Timings and express websites

The Website Starter is normally completed within 2–4 weeks after the required payment, completed questionnaire, content, images, access and approval to begin have all been received. The Express Website Set Up is completed within a maximum of five working days once those same requirements are complete and the agreed express slot begins. Delayed content, feedback, access or approvals pause the delivery timetable. Third-party platform or domain delays are outside our direct control.

Changes and cancellations

The revisions included in your project will be stated in the proposal. Additional work is quoted separately. Cancellation rights and any non-refundable work already completed will be explained before you commit.

Content, approvals and ownership

You are responsible for ensuring that supplied text, images and materials may lawfully be used. You will have an opportunity to review and approve work before launch. Ownership and licences transfer only as described in the proposal and after all related invoices are paid.

Hosting and ongoing support

Managed hosting and Website Care are ongoing services billed at the agreed interval. The plan, included updates, reasonable-use limits and cancellation arrangements will be confirmed in writing. Domains remain the client’s property.

Third-party services

Websites may rely on third-party providers such as hosting, forms, booking tools, payment processors or social platforms. We will explain relevant third-party fees, but cannot guarantee the uninterrupted availability of services outside our control.

Contact and complaints
