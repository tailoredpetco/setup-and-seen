A manageable start

What do you need before we begin?

You do not need a finished website plan. Start with your business name, the services you offer, where you work and the action you want visitors to take. We will explain what is needed for your chosen package.

  1. Your business information: service details, contact information and any prices you want to publish.
  2. Your existing brand and images: a logo if you have one, suitable photographs and permission to use them. Branding and photography requirements can be discussed separately.
  3. Your wording: Website Starter includes content guidance; the managed plan includes refinement of supplied wording. Full copywriting is included in Complete or can be discussed as a separately quoted requirement.
  4. Domain access, if you already own one: your domain remains yours. We agree the platform, ownership, editing access and launch arrangements in writing.

Questions about your first website

Can you help if we have no logo or website wording?

Yes. Tell us what you already have. Website Starter assumes branding and content can be supplied, with guidance included. Business Launch adds logo styling and refinement of wording; Complete includes brand identity and full website copywriting. We can discuss a separate quote where your needs do not fit those combinations.

What is included in the £149 monthly plan?

Managed Website Starter includes up to five pages, refinement of supplied wording, one standard .co.uk domain for the first year, hosting, technical care and one small content update of up to 30 minutes each month. The commitment is £149 a month for 12 months, £1,788 total. Full copywriting, branding, business email hosting, premium domains, paid software and complex systems are excluded. Read the full plan and exclusions.

What happens after the first 12 months?

After all 12 payments on the managed plan, you can continue with Website Care from £49 a month, subject to the service agreed at that time, or arrange a suitable transfer. Domain renewal and other paid services are separate. Ownership, cancellation and transfer terms are explained in the written agreement before you commit.

Will a new website appear at the top of Google?

No one can promise that. The website packages include basic search engine set-up, such as useful page structure, titles and descriptions. Search visibility also depends on the competition, the information on your site and other factors. Ongoing SEO work is separate unless expressly included in your proposal.

Do we need to visit an office?

No. We work with businesses across the UK by phone, video call and online. Visits to customers within 50 miles of our Worcestershire base are available by arrangement. Maria is your direct point of contact.

Can we keep our social media pages?

Yes. A website can work alongside them. Your social pages can introduce the business and share updates, while the website gives visitors fuller service information and a clear enquiry route.
