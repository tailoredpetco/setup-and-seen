Before you enquire

Useful answers.
No guesswork.

Can we start with a one-off review?+

Yes. We can begin with a focused review and practical recommendations, or quote for a specific project. You can then decide whether you want help putting the recommendations into action.

Can you be our own sales and marketing consultant?+

Yes. We can work alongside your business on agreed priorities, with one-off projects or ongoing support. Your proposal sets out the work, responsibilities, communication and review points.

How do you research competitors and networking opportunities?+

We start with your services, customers, location and goals. Competitor research uses publicly available information. Networking recommendations consider the audience, relevance, location and published costs where available. Event attendance, introductions and bookings are not included unless agreed in your proposal.

Do you create the marketing as well as recommend it?+

Yes. We can provide research and recommendations, create the agreed copy and artwork, or help deliver a wider campaign. We confirm the channels, deliverables and responsibilities before work begins.

What does it cost, and what is included?+

We quote around the agreed work rather than a fixed package. Your written proposal confirms the scope, price and delivery stages. For ongoing support, it also sets out payment arrangements, any minimum term, cancellation notice and how extra work is quoted.

Are advertising and other external costs included?+

Advertising spend is paid directly to the platform and shown separately from our planning or management fee. Event tickets, printing and third-party subscriptions are also separate unless explicitly included in your written proposal.

Do you work with businesses outside Worcestershire?+

Yes. We support small businesses across the UK remotely. Any in-person meetings, event attendance or travel are discussed and agreed separately.
